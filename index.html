1

What direction are the calls?

This shapes everything that follows — the jobs the AI handles, the volume calculation, and whether outbound charges apply.
2

Phone setup

Three ways to connect phones. The customer brings their own digital telephony (SIP), or their own mobile numbers, or we provide a landline number. A Grandstream device is required in every case, one per deployment. Own mobile numbers cannot carry concurrent calls. Carrier charges for calls are not included in any option.
3

Extensions (human agent desktops)

An extension is a desktop seat for a human agent, used in the browser. It does not limit AI capacity. One Grandstream device per deployment is added automatically.

Mobile service — gives human agents access to the platform software on their mobile phones, in addition to their desktop extensions. A human agent seat on mobile instead of desktop. 500 SAR per month flat per deployment, regardless of the number of mobiles. Available for any phone setup.

4

What should the AI do?

A flow is one type of call the AI handles. Add one flow for each type of call the customer needs, inbound and outbound. For each flow, pick the job and the systems it touches. This decides the endpoint connections in Step 6.

Outbound flows

5

Call volume & voice package

Enter daily volumes. We calculate total monthly minutes and recommend the right voice package.

6

Existing systems & endpoint connections

Built from the flows in Step 4. Each system appears once, however many flows use it. Each external system connection is 250 SAR/month — you pay per system connected, not per workflow. Unlimited workflows within each connection.

7

Concurrent calls

How many calls can happen at the same time. This is a telephony capacity charge. Each concurrent call costs 250 SAR a month. It applies to SIP and to a landline from us. It does not apply to the customer's own mobile numbers, which cannot carry concurrent calls.

8

WhatsApp

Three ways this lands, and they price very differently. WhatsApp is also an endpoint connection (250 SAR/month) in Step 6.

Tick what WhatsApp is used for. Enter how many customers or conversations a month, and how many messages each one needs.

UseConversations a monthMessages each
9

Add-ons and usage charges

Pricing rules changed for sentiment and outbound. The price may differ from your saved version.

Knowledge base (fixed monthly): not included in any package. Each knowledge base is 38 SAR a month. One knowledge base holds up to 500 URLs, 50 text snippets and 25 files (50 MB each; spreadsheets up to 1,000 rows and 50 columns). One is enough for most clients.

Inbound sentiment analysis (optional add-on, per call) — analyses the tone and sentiment of inbound calls. 1 SAR per call, counted only for calls the AI handles. Available only when inbound calls are in scope.

Outbound calls (per call) — 1 SAR per outbound call. Sentiment analysis is included, with no separate sentiment charge. Counted from the outbound calls a day and the working days in Step 5. This charge can be waived as a sales concession.

Dashboard / admin panel is required in every deal. It costs 100 SAR per user per month, with a minimum of one user. A customised dashboard is a one-time 700 SAR charge. It delivers up to 7 custom KPI charts tailored to the customer's business goal and active flows — for example, calls by hour, no-show rate trend, or cost per AI-handled call.
