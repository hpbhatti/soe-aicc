What direction are the calls?

This shapes everything that follows — the jobs the AI handles, the volume calculation, and whether outbound charges apply.
2

Phone setup

Two paths, different cost profiles. The customer brings their own mobile numbers: no Grandstream device and no minimum extensions. The customer brings their own landline number: this uses SIP and a Grandstream device per branch, and the 3-extension minimum applies. Mobile numbers are limited to one concurrent call per number (STC limitation).
3

Extensions & branches

Minimum 3 extensions (1,500 SAR/month floor). One Grandstream device per branch location.
4

What should the AI do?

A flow is one type of call the AI handles. Add one flow for each type of call the customer needs, inbound and outbound. For each flow, pick the job and the systems it touches. This decides the endpoint connections in Step 7.

5

Call volume & voice package

Enter daily volumes. We calculate total monthly minutes and recommend the right voice package.

6

Outbound contact charge

Each contact loaded into the CRM for an outbound campaign is charged at 1 SAR per contact (billed once per contact loaded, not per call attempt). The attempts selector below is for volume cross-checking only — it does not multiply the cost. This charge can be waived as a sales concession.

7

Existing systems & endpoint connections

Built from the flows in Step 4. Each system appears once, however many flows use it. Each external system connection is 250 SAR/month — you pay per system connected, not per workflow. Unlimited workflows within each connection.

8

Concurrent calls

How many calls can happen at the same time. This is a telephony capacity charge, separate from extensions. Each concurrent call costs 250 SAR a month. Cannot exceed the number of extensions (agent seats) set in Step 3. For mobile setups, concurrency is also limited to the number of mobile numbers.

9

WhatsApp

Three ways this lands, and they price very differently. WhatsApp is also an endpoint connection (250 SAR/month) in Step 7.

Tick what WhatsApp is used for. Enter how many customers or conversations a month, and how many messages each one needs.

10

Add-ons

Knowledge base: not included in any package. Each knowledge base is 38 SAR a month. One knowledge base holds up to 500 URLs, 50 text snippets and 25 files (50 MB each; spreadsheets up to 1,000 rows and 50 columns). One is enough for most clients.

11

700Apps CRM optional upsell

If they need a CRM and chose "Use 700Apps CRM" in Step 7, this is where the subscription is configured. Taking the 700Apps CRM drops the CRM endpoint connection to zero.

12

How to present it

Free months come off the recurring cost only. Setup and hardware are always billed in full.
